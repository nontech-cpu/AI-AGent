Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 17, 2026 • Production URL: https://peculiarai.shapebytes.com/privacy • Application: PeculiarAI

At PeculiarAI (referred to as "we", "our", or "the Service"), accessible from https://peculiarai.shapebytes.com, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data practices, detailing what information is collected, how it is used, how Google Workspace data is processed, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our multi-channel conversational assistant:

  • Phone Number & OTP Data: When initiating authentication via our web interface, you provide your phone number. A temporary verification token (expiring in 600 seconds) is generated to match verification requests with our Telegram verification bot (@PeculiarAI_Verify_Bot).
  • Telegram Information: Your Telegram User ID and conversation prompts when interacting with PeculiarAI bots.
  • Conversational Chat Queries: Text messages and prompts submitted through the web interface (/web/chat) or Telegram bot to generate real-time AI responses.
  • Technical Logs: Server logs including IP addresses, browser user-agent, request timestamps, and response status codes for system performance monitoring, debugging, and abuse prevention.

2. Google User Data & OAuth Scopes

PeculiarAI offers optional integrations with Google Workspace to facilitate scheduling and email drafting. When you explicitly connect your Google Account, we request access strictly to the following scopes:

A. Google Calendar Events (calendar.events)

Scope URI: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events
Data Accessed: Calendar event titles, start/end dates and times, event descriptions, and attendee lists.
Purpose: To check your availability and schedule, update, or organize calendar appointments when you explicitly ask the assistant to schedule an event.

B. Gmail Compose (gmail.compose)

Scope URI: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.compose
Data Accessed: Outgoing email subject, recipient address, and message body drafted upon your request.
Purpose: To compose email drafts or dispatch messages per your explicit instruction. PeculiarAI does not read your personal inbox archive or request full mailbox access.

C. Basic Identity (openid, userinfo.email)

Purpose: To verify your Google account identity and link your authorized integration to your PeculiarAI profile.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

PeculiarAI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • No Selling of Data: We never sell Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.
  • No Advertising: Google user data is never used or transferred for serving advertisements, retargeting, or interest-based profiling.
  • No AI Model Training on User Data: Google user data received through Google APIs is never used to train, retrain, or improve generalized or foundational artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Human Oversight Restrictions: Human beings do not read your Google user data unless: (a) you have provided explicit consent for specific troubleshooting, (b) it is necessary for security purposes (such as investigating abuse), or (c) required by applicable law.

4. Storage, Encryption & Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical controls to safeguard user information:

  • Encrypted OAuth Tokens: Google OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using strong AES encryption on our backend servers. Tokens are never exposed to frontend JavaScript or client-side storage.
  • HTTPS Transport: All data transmitted between your browser, our API backend, and external providers is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Ephemeral OTP Verification: Verification tokens generated during phone authentication expire automatically after 10 minutes (600 seconds) and are permanently invalidated once used.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide the active services:

  • Session & Chat Data: Web chat messages are kept temporarily during active sessions. Users can clear local conversation history at any time using the "Clear" button in the dashboard.
  • Disconnection & Revocation: You can disconnect your Google integration at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions. Upon revocation, our backend will permanently purge your stored tokens upon request.
  • Account Deletion: To request complete deletion of your associated phone number, chat records, and integration credentials, email us at support@peculiarai.shapebytes.com. Requests are processed within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted infrastructure providers to deliver the PeculiarAI service:

  • Cloud Hosting & Compute: Fast server hosting providing API execution and encrypted database persistence.
  • Telegram Bot API: Telegram FZ-LLC for delivering authentication codes and hosting interactive conversational bot interfaces.
  • AI Model Inference Providers: Enterprise AI processing APIs to generate text responses. Prompt data sent to inference providers is processed under zero-data-retention and non-training terms.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

PeculiarAI does not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels. We utilize standard browser localStorage solely for functional preferences: saving your dark/light theme choice, your local session ID, and your verification state.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technical enhancements or regulatory changes. The current version will always be maintained at https://peculiarai.shapebytes.com/privacy with the effective revision date indicated at the top.
